# History days prototype verdict

Question: how should History days appear in the library and on the map?

The owner picked **A (Section below tracks)**:

- A collapsible "Google history" section below the track list, with an "Inferred" badge.
- One "Show on map" toggle for all history, a checkbox per year group and per day.
- Day rows show place names first and the date second.
- Map: 2 px translucent lines in one shared muted colour, drawn under tracks.
- Selected day: a stronger line, place-visit stops, and a panel with time span, kept and dropped fixes, and place visits that pan the map.

B (tabs and calendar heatmap) and C (inline year rows and dots) were rejected. The synthetic paths are random walks, so line shapes say nothing about real data.
